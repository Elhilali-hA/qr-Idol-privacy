
# Privacy Policy — QR Idol

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

QR Idol ("the App") is a QR code scanning and generation application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use it.

## 1. Information We Access

### Camera
QR Idol requests camera access so you can scan QR codes. Camera access is used to detect and read QR codes. The App does not intentionally record or store camera video.

### Photos and Images
If you choose to scan a QR code from an image, QR Idol requests access to your selected image. The image is processed to detect QR code content.

The App does not intentionally upload your selected images to our servers.

### QR Code Content
QR Idol processes the information contained in QR codes, such as website addresses, text, contact information, and Wi-Fi configuration details.

QR content is processed locally on your device for scanning and display. Be careful when opening links or sharing information obtained from QR codes.

## 2. Local Storage and Scan History

QR Idol may store your scan history and app preferences locally on your device.

This information is stored using local device storage and is not sent to a QR Idol server.

You can delete individual history entries or clear your scan history using the features provided in the App. Uninstalling the App may also remove locally stored app data.

## 3. QR Code Generation

QR Idol allows you to generate QR codes from information you enter, including text, website addresses, and Wi-Fi details.

The information you enter is processed on your device to generate the QR code. The App does not intentionally send this information to a QR Idol server.

If you generate a Wi-Fi QR code, the network name and password you enter are encoded into the QR code. Anyone who can scan that QR code may be able to access the encoded information. Only share it with people you trust.

## 5. Analytics and Tracking

QR Idol does not currently use analytics services such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

The App does not intentionally track users across other apps or websites.

This section will be updated if these practices change.

## 6. Sharing of Information

QR Idol does not operate its own server to collect or sell your QR content or scan history.

If you choose to use your device's sharing features, information you select may be shared with the application or person you choose.

Third-party services integrated into future versions, such as advertising providers, may process information as described in this policy and their own privacy policies.

## 7. Data Security and Retention

Information stored locally remains on your device and is subject to your device's security settings.

We do not maintain a QR Idol online account or cloud database for your scan history.

You can remove locally stored history through the App. You may also uninstall the App to remove its local data, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings.
